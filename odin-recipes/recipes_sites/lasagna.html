Lasagna

Description

An easy lasagna recipe that saves me lots of time. This dish satisfies everyone in the family. Try it tonight!

  • 1 (12 inch) pre-baked pizza crust
  • ½ cup pesto
  • ripe tomato, chopped
  • ½ cup green bell pepper, chopped
  1. wash your hands
  2. heat your oven to 375°F
  3. in a large bowl, mix together the pesto, tomato, and green bell pepper
  4. spread the mixture onto the pre-baked pizza crust
  5. bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted
  6. let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving
