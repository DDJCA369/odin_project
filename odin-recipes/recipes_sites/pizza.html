Pizza

Description

This pesto pizza can be made with your favorite prepared pesto or homemade pesto. It's so quick to make for a light and flavorful alternative to regular cheese and tomato pizza!

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 4 ounces marinara sauce, divided
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  1. wash your hands
  2. preheat your oven to 450°F (230°C)
  3. roll out the dough on a floured surface
  4. spread the sauce over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges
  5. top with your favorite toppings
  6. bake for 15-20 minutes or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted
